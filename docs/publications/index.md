# Publications

This section contains the latest research publications, papers, and academic works in the field of evolvable hardware, with a focus on **FPGA-intrinsic analog evolution** and those produced in collaboration with the open source evolvable hardware community.

## Foundational Publications

### Seminal Papers in Evolvable Hardware

**Thompson, A. (1996).** "An evolved circuit, intrinsic in silicon, entwined with physics." *Proceedings of the First International Conference on Evolvable Systems*, 390-405.

- **Impact**: Established FPGA-intrinsic evolution methodology
- **Key contribution**: Demonstrated evolution exploiting physical device properties
- **Historical significance**: Canonical example of evolvable hardware
- **Relevance**: Foundation for our current iCE40-based research

**Thompson, A. (1998).** "Hardware evolution: Automatic design of electronic circuits in reconfigurable hardware by artificial evolution." *Springer-Verlag Distinguished Dissertations*, ISBN: 978-3-540-62003-0.

- **Comprehensive coverage**: Complete methodology for hardware evolution
- **Experimental validation**: Multiple successful analog circuit experiments  
- **Technical depth**: Detailed bitstream evolution techniques
- **Legacy**: Established theoretical framework still used today

**Higuchi, T., Iwata, M., & Liu, W. (Eds.) (2006).** "Evolvable Hardware: From Practice to Application." *Springer-Verlag*, ISBN: 978-3-540-31312-8.

- **Field overview**: Comprehensive survey of evolvable hardware research
- **Multiple perspectives**: Contributors from academia and industry
- **Applications focus**: Real-world implementations and case studies
- **Educational value**: Essential reference for newcomers to the field

## Historical Publications Archive

### Critical Papers from the XC6200 Era (1996-2000)

**de Garis, H. (1993).** "Evolvable hardware: Genetic programming of a Darwin machine." *Proceedings of ICES*, 441-449.
- **Historical significance**: Coined the term "evolvable hardware"
- **Vision**: Predicted evolution-based circuit design
- **Influence**: Inspired entire field of research

**Miller, J. F., Job, D., & Vassilev, V. K. (2000).** "Principles in the evolutionary design of digital circuits." *Genetic Programming and Evolvable Machines*, 1(1-2), 7-35.
- **Digital evolution**: Focus on digital circuit evolution
- **Methodology**: Systematic approach to circuit representation
- **Tools**: Early software platforms for circuit evolution

**Lohn, J. D., & Colombano, S. P. (1998).** "Automated analog circuit synthesis using a linear representation." *Proceedings of ICES*, 125-133.
- **NASA contribution**: Space applications focus
- **Linear representation**: Alternative to bitstream evolution
- **Applications**: Practical circuits for space missions

### The "Silent Period" (2000-2015)

**Research Focus Shift**: After the XC6200 discontinuation, research largely moved to:
- **Simulation-based evolution** (extrinsic approaches)
- **Digital circuit evolution** using higher-level representations
- **Alternative platforms** (FPAAs, custom silicon)
- **Theoretical studies** and algorithmic improvements

**Key Publications During This Period:**

**Sekanina, L. (2004).** "Evolvable components: From theory to hardware implementations." *Springer Natural Computing Series*.
- **Alternative approaches**: Non-FPGA evolvable systems
- **Theoretical foundations**: Advanced evolutionary techniques
- **Implementation studies**: Various hardware platforms

**Greenwood, G. W., & Tyrrell, A. M. (2007).** "Introduction to evolvable hardware: A practical guide for designing self-adaptive systems." *Wiley-IEEE Press*.
- **Educational focus**: Practical guide for researchers
- **Broader perspective**: Multiple evolutionary approaches
- **System design**: Self-adaptive system principles

## Current Research Directions

- Balancing performance, power consumption, and area
- Pareto-optimal circuit design exploration
- Application to IoT and edge computing devices

**Distributed Evolution Across Multiple FPGAs**
- Population migration strategies
- Parallel fitness evaluation
- Collaborative circuit design

**Integration with Machine Learning**
- Neural network-guided evolution
- Transfer learning for circuit design
- Automated fitness function generation

**Environmental Adaptation Studies**
- Temperature compensation circuits
