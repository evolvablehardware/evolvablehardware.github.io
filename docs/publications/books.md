# Books and Monographs

## Foundational Texts

### "Evolvable Hardware: From Practice to Application"
**Authors:** Tetsuya Higuchi, Masaya Iwata, Weixin Liu  
**Publisher:** Springer-Verlag  
**Year:** 2006  
**ISBN:** 978-3-540-31312-8

The seminal text that established evolvable hardware as a distinct research discipline. Covers theoretical foundations, practical implementations, and real-world applications. Essential reading for anyone entering the field.

**Key Topics:**
- Historical development of evolvable hardware
- Evolutionary algorithms for hardware design
- FPGA-based evolution platforms
- NASA space applications
- Industrial case studies

## Acquisition and Access

### Academic Libraries

Most university libraries provide access through:
- **SpringerLink**: Comprehensive Springer publications
- **IEEE Xplore**: Conference proceedings and journals
- **ACM Digital Library**: Computing-focused publications
- **Wiley Online Library**: Engineering and technology texts

### Individual Purchase

**Student Discounts**: Available from most publishers
**E-Book Options**: Often more affordable than print versions  
**Rental Services**: Short-term access for specific projects
**Used Book Markets**: Cost-effective options for classic texts

### Open Access Resources

**arXiv.org**: Preprints and working papers
**ResearchGate**: Author-shared publications
**Institutional Repositories**: University-hosted publications
**Government Publications**: NASA and DoD technical reports

### Interlibrary Loan

Most academic institutions provide interlibrary loan services for accessing books not available locally. Processing typically takes 1-2 weeks.
