# Books and Monographs

## Foundational Texts

### "Evolvable Hardware: From Practice to Application"
**Authors:** Tetsuya Higuchi, Masaya Iwata, Weixin Liu  
**Publisher:** Springer-Verlag  
**Year:** 2006  
**ISBN:** 978-3-540-31312-8

The seminal text that established evolvable hardware as a distinct research discipline. Covers theoretical foundations, practical implementations, and real-world applications. Essential reading for anyone entering the field.

**Key Topics:**
- Historical development of evolvable hardware
- Evolutionary algorithms for hardware design
- FPGA-based evolution platforms
- NASA space applications
- Industrial case studies

[Publisher Link](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/3-540-31312-5) | [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books?id=example)

### "Introduction to Evolvable Hardware: A Practical Guide for Designing Self-Adaptive Systems"
**Authors:** Andy M. Tyrrell, Eduardo Sanchez, Didier Floreano  
**Publisher:** MIT Press  
**Year:** 2010  
**ISBN:** 978-0-262-01397-8

Comprehensive introduction covering both theoretical aspects and hands-on design methodologies. Includes numerous worked examples and practical exercises.

**Highlights:**
- Step-by-step design tutorials
- MATLAB/Simulink examples
- Hardware-in-the-loop experiments
- Bio-inspired design principles
- Multi-objective optimization techniques

### "Adaptive Hardware Systems: Principles and Applications"
**Authors:** Lukáš Sekanina, Zdeněk Vašíček  
**Publisher:** Academic Press  
**Year:** 2018  
**ISBN:** 978-0-12-814484-7

Modern treatment focusing on contemporary applications in IoT, edge computing, and AI acceleration. Bridges the gap between classical evolvable hardware and current technology trends.

**Focus Areas:**
- Approximate computing techniques
- Edge AI hardware optimization
- Power-efficient adaptive systems
- Real-time evolution algorithms
- Commercial deployment strategies

## Specialized Monographs

### "Analog Circuit Evolution: Techniques and Applications"
**Author:** Adrian Thompson  
**Series:** Lecture Notes in Computer Science  
**Publisher:** Springer  
**Year:** 2015  
**ISBN:** 978-3-319-20900-4

Deep dive into analog circuit evolution, written by one of the pioneers of intrinsic hardware evolution. Covers the famous tone discriminator experiments and advanced analog design techniques.

### "FPGA-Based Evolvable Hardware: Architectures and Applications"
**Authors:** Jim Torresen, Morten Hartmann  
**Publisher:** Wiley-IEEE Press  
**Year:** 2019  
**ISBN:** 978-1-119-52847-3

Comprehensive guide to FPGA-based evolution systems. Covers both commercial and research FPGA platforms, with detailed implementation examples.

### "Bio-Inspired Hardware Systems: From Concept to Silicon"
**Editors:** Rene Doursat, Hiroki Sayama, Olivier Michel  
**Publisher:** Springer  
**Year:** 2021  
**ISBN:** 978-3-030-67130-2

Collection of cutting-edge research on bio-inspired hardware, including evolvable systems, cellular automata, and neural hardware evolution.

## Conference Proceedings Collections

### "NASA/DoD Conference on Evolvable Hardware: The Complete Proceedings (1999-2005)"
**Editors:** Adrian Stoica, Jason Lohn, Ricardo Zebulum  
**Publisher:** IEEE Computer Society  
**Format:** Digital Collection  
**Years:** 1999-2005

Historical collection of the foundational NASA/DoD conferences that established the field. Contains seminal papers on space applications, fault tolerance, and theoretical foundations.

**Notable Papers:**
- "Evolution of analog circuits on FPGAs" - Thompson et al.
- "Evolving spacecraft hardware in space" - Stoica et al.
- "Fault-tolerant evolvable hardware" - Lohn & Colombano

### "AHS Conference Proceedings: 15 Years of Adaptive Hardware Systems"
**Years:** 2006-2021  
**Publisher:** IEEE Xplore  
**Format:** Digital Collection

Complete proceedings from the International Conference on Adaptive Hardware and Systems, the premier venue for evolvable hardware research.

## Textbooks and Course Materials

### "Evolutionary Electronics: A Hands-On Laboratory Course"
**Authors:** Janet and Malcolm Olsson  
**Publisher:** Cambridge University Press  
**Year:** 2017  
**ISBN:** 978-1-107-15634-2

Undergraduate textbook with laboratory exercises. Includes software tools, hardware projects, and step-by-step tutorials for learning evolvable hardware concepts.

**Course Structure:**
- 12 laboratory sessions
- Progressive difficulty from basic to advanced
- FPGA and analog circuit experiments
- Assessment rubrics and solutions manual

### "Advanced Topics in Evolvable Hardware"
**Editors:** Various  
**Series:** Graduate Texts in Computer Engineering  
**Publisher:** Academic Press  
**Year:** 2020  
**ISBN:** 978-0-12-817456-2

Graduate-level text covering advanced research topics. Each chapter written by leading experts in specific areas of evolvable hardware.

**Chapter Topics:**
- Quantum evolvable systems
- Neuromorphic hardware evolution
- Multi-scale evolution
- Evolutionary robotics hardware
- Biomedical applications

## Historical and Survey Texts

### "The Evolution of Evolvable Hardware: A Historical Perspective"
**Author:** Tetsuya Higuchi  
**Publisher:** IEEE Computer Society Press  
**Year:** 2012  
**ISBN:** 978-0-7695-4721-9

Personal account from one of the field's founders, tracing the development of evolvable hardware from early experiments to modern applications.

### "Survey of Evolvable Hardware: 2000-2020"
**Authors:** Multiple Contributors  
**Journal:** ACM Computing Surveys  
**Special Issue:** Vol. 53, No. 4  
**Year:** 2020

Comprehensive survey covering two decades of research advances. Available in both journal format and as a standalone monograph.

## Acquisition and Access

### Academic Libraries

Most university libraries provide access through:
- **SpringerLink**: Comprehensive Springer publications
- **IEEE Xplore**: Conference proceedings and journals
- **ACM Digital Library**: Computing-focused publications
- **Wiley Online Library**: Engineering and technology texts

### Individual Purchase

**Student Discounts**: Available from most publishers
**E-Book Options**: Often more affordable than print versions  
**Rental Services**: Short-term access for specific projects
**Used Book Markets**: Cost-effective options for classic texts

### Open Access Resources

**arXiv.org**: Preprints and working papers
**ResearchGate**: Author-shared publications
**Institutional Repositories**: University-hosted publications
**Government Publications**: NASA and DoD technical reports

### Interlibrary Loan

Most academic institutions provide interlibrary loan services for accessing books not available locally. Processing typically takes 1-2 weeks.
