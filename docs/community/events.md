## How to Participate

### Conference Attendance
1. **Registration:** Early bird discounts available
2. **Presentations:** Submit papers or posters
3. **Networking:** Attend social events and workshops
4. **Follow-up:** Maintain connections made

### Speaking Opportunities
1. **Call for Papers:** Submit research contributions
2. **Workshops:** Propose specialized sessions
3. **Invited Talks:** Nominations by community members
4. **Panels:** Industry and policy discussions

### Organizing Events
1. **Local Workshops:** Host regional events
2. **Special Sessions:** Organize conference tracks
3. **Competition Judging:** Evaluate student work
4. **Mentorship:** Guide newcomers to the field
