# Community Members

## Get Involved

The **Evolvable Hardware community is growing!** Join the movement, participate in the research, or hack the gibson! Whether you're interested in the science, engineering, or tinkering, you're welcome here!

### Slack Channel
Our group is very active and communicates on a daily basis using [Slack](https://slack.com/). We have channels in place for different methods, focus areas, and other general interests.

**To join our workspace:** Send an email to [derek.whitley1@gmail.com](mailto:derek.whitley1@gmail.com) with the subject line **"EHW SLACK"** to receive an invite.

**Active channels include:**
- `#general` - Community discussions and announcements
- `#experiments` - Sharing experimental results and troubleshooting
- `#hardware` - FPGA setup, debugging, and hardware discussions  
- `#software` - Code sharing, GA implementation, and tool development
- `#theory` - Research discussions and literature
- `#random` - Off-topic conversations and community building

### GitHub Organization
We are keeping this project completely open-source to aid in the further development and continuity of this research domain. All code is available at [github.com/evolvablehardware](https://github.com/evolvablehardware).

**Ways to contribute:**
- **Testing and debugging**: Help validate experimental setups
- **Feature additions**: Implement new evolution algorithms or fitness functions
- **New experiment ideas**: Propose novel applications of evolvable hardware
- **Hardware contributions**: Design custom boards or interfaces
- **Documentation**: Improve guides, tutorials, and examples
- **General criticism**: Provide feedback on methodologies and approaches

Like most open-source projects, it is constantly under development. We welcome any and all levels of developers, engineers, researchers that are interested in making contributions to the research.

## Research Groups and Laboratories

### Core Research Team
**Primary experimental platform development**

Our core team focuses on FPGA-intrinsic analog evolvable hardware using the Lattice iCE40 platform. Current research areas include:

- Circuit evolution methodology development
- Fitness function design and optimization
- Hardware platform optimization
- Open-source toolchain development

## Community Guidelines

### Inclusion and Diversity
The evolvable hardware community is inclusive, diverse, and welcoming to researchers from all backgrounds. We encourage participation from underrepresented groups and support initiatives for equity and inclusion in STEM.

**Our values:**
- **Open collaboration**: Share knowledge freely and openly
- **Respectful discourse**: Maintain professional and constructive communication
- **Inclusive participation**: Welcome contributors of all experience levels
- **Diversity celebration**: Value different perspectives and backgrounds
- **Learning culture**: Support continuous learning and skill development

### Code of Conduct
**Professional Standards**
- Be respectful and professional in all interactions
- Provide constructive feedback and criticism
- Acknowledge contributions and give proper credit
- Maintain confidentiality when appropriate
- Follow open-source licensing requirements

**Communication Guidelines**
- Use clear, professional language in all channels
- Stay on-topic in specialized channels
- Be patient with new community members
- Share resources and help others learn
- Report inappropriate behavior to moderators

### Recognition and Awards

**Community Contributions**
We recognize outstanding contributions to the evolvable hardware community:

- **Technical contributions**: Novel algorithms, hardware designs, or experimental results
- **Educational contributions**: Tutorials, documentation, or teaching materials  
- **Community building**: Organizing events, mentoring, or outreach activities
- **Open source contributions**: Code, datasets, or tools shared with the community

**Annual Recognition**
- Outstanding Researcher Award
- Best Educational Content Award  
- Community Builder Award
- Innovation in Evolvable Hardware Award

## Getting Started Guide

### For New Researchers
1. **Join the Slack workspace** for daily community interaction
2. **Review our [hardware setup guide](../resources/hardware.md)** to get started
3. **Try the variance maximization experiment** as your first project
4. **Share your results** and get feedback from the community
5. **Contribute improvements** to tools and documentation

### For Students
1. **Explore our [educational resources](../resources/education.md)** and tutorials
2. **Connect with academic mentors** through our community network
3. **Participate in student research programs** and competitions
4. **Present your work** at conferences and community events
5. **Build your portfolio** with open-source contributions

### For Industry Partners
1. **Contact us directly** to discuss collaboration opportunities
2. **Sponsor research initiatives** or community events
3. **Provide hardware** or financial support for projects
4. **Engage students** through internships and job opportunities
5. **License technology** developed by the community
