???+ abstract
    This book describes how artificial evolution has been used to design electronics automatically. The availability of reconfigurable silicon chips has made it possible for artificial evolution to work with real, physical electronic media, rather than just software simulations. The main thesis is that evolution can explore beyond the realms of conventional circuit design, finding solutions that conventional design methodologies cannot, in the process revealing new insights into the physics of semiconductor devices. One notable experiment described involves a circuit evolved to distinguish between signals of two different frequencies.
