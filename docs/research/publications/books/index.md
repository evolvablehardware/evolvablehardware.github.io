# Books

Below are books that are relevant to the field of Evolvable Hardware. These are recommended reading for anyone looking to deepen their understanding of the field.

-   [__Evolvable Hardware: From Practice to Application__](./2015_evolvable-hardware-practice-to-application.md) <br>
    _Martin A. Trefzer, Andy M. Tyrrell — Springer, 2015_

-   [__Hardware Evolution: Automatic Design of Electronic Circuits in Reconfigurable Hardware by Artificial Evolution__](./1998_hardware-evolution-thompson.md) <br>
    _Adrian Thompson — Springer, 1998_
