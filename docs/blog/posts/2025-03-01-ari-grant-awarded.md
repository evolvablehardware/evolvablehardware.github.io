# ARI Grant Awarded for Partnership with Vivum AI and Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology

We are excited to announce that we have been awarded a grant from the Applied Research Institute, Inc (ARI) which will serve in part to support our ongoing research in evolvable hardware.

<!-- more -->

This grant will allow us to continue developing and refining our technology, as well as explore new applications and collaborations in the field. We are grateful for the support from ARI and look forward to the opportunities this grant will provide for our research.
