### September 2025

**Tone Discrimination Experiment Shows Promising Results** 
*September 25, 2025*

Our ongoing recreation of Thompson's famous tone discriminator is showing encouraging progress. Recent generations have demonstrated clear frequency-dependent responses, with some individuals showing preliminary discrimination between 1kHz and 10kHz input tones.

![Tone Evolution Progress](../evolvablehardware.org/images/toneevolution.png)

**Key developments:**
- Generation 67 achieved 73% classification accuracy
- Clear frequency-selective responses observed in evolved circuits
- Novel circuit topologies discovered using unconventional FPGA resources
- Real-time fitness evaluation system working reliably

**Next steps:** Extended evolution runs targeting >90% accuracy threshold.

## Experimental Milestones

### June 2025

**Pulse Oscillation Experiment Successfully Completed**
*June 18, 2025*

Another milestone achieved! Our recreation of Thompson's pulse generation experiment has successfully evolved stable oscillatory circuits operating at biological timescales.

![Pulse Results](../evolvablehardware.org/images/pulseevolution.png)

**Results summary:**
- **Target achieved:** 1ms period oscillations evolved
- **Stability:** Circuits maintained operation for >24 hours
- **Efficiency:** Using only 42 configurable logic blocks (like Thompson's original)
- **Reproducibility:** 8 independent runs all achieved stable oscillation

**Research impact:** Validates our platform's ability to recreate classic EHW experiments on modern hardware.

---

### May 2025

**First Multi-FPGA Evolution Experiment**
*May 10, 2025*

Pioneer community member successfully demonstrated distributed evolution across 4 synchronized iCE40 FPGAs:

- **Population distribution:** 50 individuals across 4 FPGAs (12-13 each)
- **Migration strategy:** Best individuals shared every 10 generations
- **Performance gain:** 3.2x speedup in evolution time
- **Novel insights:** Population diversity benefits from distributed approach

**Open source:** Complete setup and code available on GitHub
