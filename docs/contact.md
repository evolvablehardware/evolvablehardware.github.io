## Community Guidelines

When contacting us or participating in community discussions:

1. **Be respectful** - Maintain professional and courteous communication
2. **Stay on topic** - Focus on evolvable hardware and related research
3. **Share knowledge** - Contribute to the collective understanding
4. **Cite sources** - Give proper attribution to research and ideas
5. **Welcome newcomers** - Help build an inclusive research community
