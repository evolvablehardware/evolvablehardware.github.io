# Contact

Get in touch with the evolvable hardware community and find ways to collaborate with researchers worldwide.

## Get Involved

The Evolvable Hardware community is growing! Join the movement, participate in the research, or hack the gibson! Whether you're interested in the science, engineering, or tinkering, **you're welcome here!**

### Join Our Slack Workspace

Our group is very active and communicates on a daily basis using [Slack](https://slack.com/). We have channels for different methods, focus areas, and general interests.

📧 **To get access**: Send an email to [derek.whitley1@gmail.com](mailto:derek.whitley1@gmail.com) with the subject line **"EHW SLACK"**

**Active channels include:**
- `#general` - Community discussions and announcements  
- `#experiments` - Sharing experimental results and troubleshooting
- `#hardware` - FPGA setup, debugging, and hardware discussions
- `#software` - Code sharing, GA implementation, and tool development  
- `#theory` - Research discussions and literature
- `#random` - Off-topic conversations and community building

## Open Source Collaboration

### GitHub Organization
We are keeping this project completely **open-source** to aid in the further development and continuity of evolvable hardware research.

� **All code available at**: [github.com/evolvablehardware](https://github.com/evolvablehardware)

**We welcome contributors at all levels:**
- **Testing and debugging**: Help validate experimental setups
- **Feature development**: Implement new evolution algorithms or fitness functions  
- **New experiment ideas**: Propose novel applications of evolvable hardware
- **Novel hardware**: Design custom boards or measurement interfaces
- **General criticisms**: Provide feedback on methodologies and approaches

Like most open-source projects, it is constantly under development. Whether you're a developer, engineer, or researcher, your contributions are valued!

## Specific Areas of Interest

### Current Research Focus
- **FPGA-intrinsic analog evolution** using iCE40 platform
- **Circuit evolution methodology** development
- **Fitness function design** and optimization  
- **Hardware platform** optimization
- **Open-source toolchain** development

### Future Research Directions
- **Multi-objective optimization** for evolved circuits
- **Distributed evolution** across multiple FPGAs
- **Reservoir computing** using FPGA fabric
- **Environmental adaptation** and robustness testing
- **Integration with machine learning** systems
